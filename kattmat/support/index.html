My reminders don’t come

Reminders come with the free trial or a plan. Before that there are none. In your partner’s household, your reminders follow their plan. Already have a trial or a plan? Check these:

  • The routine has a time and “Remind me” is on.
  • Your iPhone’s Settings let Kattmat send notifications.
  • No Focus is holding them back.
  • Today doesn’t say “Reminders are off”. If it does, tap “Turn on”.
  • No sitter is looking after your pets. On a sitter’s days the reminders go to them instead. You can change this with “Pause my reminders” when you invite them.

Already marked it done today? Then it waits until the next day it’s due.

Stopping a routine

Tap the routine. Then tap “Edit routine” and “Delete routine”. If it has history, you get two choices:

  • “Stop routine” takes it off Today and stops its reminders. History keeps its days. If you marked it done today, it stays on Today until tomorrow.
  • “Delete routine and its history” removes it all.

Widgets and Siri

Touch and hold your Home Screen. Tap “Edit” and then “Add Widget”. Search for Kattmat.

Tap a check in the widget to mark a routine done. Tapped the wrong one? Tap it again within five seconds to undo.

You can also ask Siri “What’s left today in Kattmat?” The Shortcuts app lists everything Siri can do.

A new iPhone

Is “Keep in iCloud” on? Then your household comes along by itself. There’s nothing to move by hand:

  1. Sign in to the new iPhone with the same Apple Account.
  2. Install Kattmat and open it.
  3. Tap Settings (the sliders at the top left of Today). Turn on “Keep in iCloud”.

Your pets and routines appear within a minute. Keep Kattmat open until they do.

“Keep in iCloud” is off?

Then move your household in a backup file:

  1. On the old iPhone, tap “Back up household” in Settings.
  2. Save the file in Files or send it to yourself.
  3. On the new iPhone, open the file. Or tap “Restore from a file…” in Settings.

Kattmat shows what’s in the file before anything changes.

Your plan comes too

Your plan belongs to your Apple Account, not to your household. So Kattmat finds it by itself on any iPhone signed in with that account. It doesn’t matter how you moved. If your plan doesn’t show, tap it at the top of Settings. Then tap “Restore purchases”.

Sharing with a partner or sitter

Open Settings. Under People, tap “Invite your partner” or “Invite a sitter”. Then tap “Send invite…”

For a sitter you pick a “First day” and a “Last day”. Their access ends by itself after the last day.

The person you invite needs an iPhone with Kattmat installed. They also need an Apple Account. Joining is free for them. Inviting needs the free trial or a plan.

A sitter without an iPhone

Send them a checklist PDF instead. On the Pets tab, tap ⋯ and then “Sitter checklist…”

Your sitter fills it in on a screen. They can use Files on an iPhone or iPad, Google Drive on Android, or Chrome or Edge on a computer. Then they save it and send the same file back to you. A photo or screenshot of it can’t be read.

Open the returned file in Kattmat. It shows you what it found before anything goes into History. Making a checklist needs the trial or a plan. Reading one back is always free.

What Kattmat costs

Kattmat is free to download. Setting up your pets and routines is free too.

Marking routines done and reminders start with the free trial. It gives you 14 days of everything, once per Apple Account. It starts when you choose the monthly or yearly plan. After the trial, keep Kattmat with one of these:

  • Monthly $3.99 · €3.99 · 49 kr a month
  • Yearly $29.99 · €29.99 · 349 kr a year
  • Lifetime $49.99 · €49.99 · 599 kr, paid once

Every plan has everything, for as many pets as you like. The App Store shows your exact price before you buy.

Partners and sitters you invite use Kattmat for free. If your plan ends, nothing is deleted. Marking done and reminders stop, but you can still see it all and back it up. Widgets still show your day, and Siri still tells you what’s left. In a TestFlight beta, everything is unlocked while you test.

Cancel, restore or refund

Cancel: In Settings, tap your plan at the top. Tap “Manage subscription” and then “Cancel Subscription”. Cancel at least two days before the trial ends, and you pay nothing.

Restore: In Settings, tap your plan. Then tap “Restore purchases”.

Refund: Refunds go through Apple. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com. Tap “I’d like to” and then “Request a refund”. Choose Kattmat.

Your household stays on your iPhone. It goes to iCloud only if you turn that on or share it. We collect nothing. Read more in the privacy policy.
