Kattmat support

Meals and care for every cat, on iPhone

Hi, Ruby here. Something not working, or an idea for Kattmat? Write to kattmat.app@icloud.com, in English or Swedish. A human answers, usually within a few days (I supervise).

It helps to say which iPhone you have, its iOS version, and what you tapped just before.

How do I start?

Kattmat starts empty. Tap Add a pet on Today, give them a name and tap Add, then add their routines on their page: meals, play, the litter box, whatever your home runs on. Each routine shows on Today on the days it's due. Setting up is free; ticking things off, reminders, widgets and Siri start with the free trial.

My reminders don't come

Reminders come during the free trial and with Kattmat. Before the trial they wait, and they pause a few days after a plan ends. Check that the routine has a time, that Kattmat may send notifications (Settings › Notifications › Kattmat on your iPhone), and that a Focus isn't holding them back. If Today says Reminders are off, tap Turn on. A routine you've already marked done today won't remind you again until tomorrow.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for Kattmat. There are three Home Screen sizes, Lock Screen widgets, and a control for Control Center. Tap a routine's check (or Done) in the widget to mark it done; tap again to undo. On a locked iPhone, marking a routine done, undoing a tick or logging a meal from a widget, Control Center, Siri or a reminder asks for Face ID first.

What can I ask Siri?

Try "What's left today in Kattmat?", "What's next in Kattmat?" or "Tick off the next one in Kattmat". The Shortcuts app lists them all.

How do I keep my data safe, or move to a new iPhone?

In Kattmat, open Settings (top left of Today) and tap Back up household. Save the file in Files or send it to yourself. On the new iPhone, open the file, or use Restore from a file… in Settings; Kattmat shows what's in it before anything changes.

Or turn on Keep in iCloud in Settings on each iPhone, and your household follows your Apple Account to all of them.

How do I share with a partner or a sitter?

Open Settings › People and tap Invite your partner or Invite a sitter (for a sitter, pick their first and last day), then Send invite…. Sharing goes through iCloud, so inviting turns on Keep in iCloud. Inviting needs Kattmat (or the free trial); once your partner has joined, they can invite a sitter too. The person needs an iPhone with Kattmat and an Apple Account, and joining is free for them. A sitter's access ends after their last day.

A sitter without Kattmat can get a checklist PDF to fill in in Google Drive, Chrome or Edge, or on paper: on the Pets tab, tap ⋯ › Sitter checklist…. When the filled-in copy comes back, open it in Kattmat, or use Import sitter checklist… in Settings; Kattmat shows what it found before adding it to History.

What does Kattmat cost?

Kattmat is free to download, and setting up your cats is free. You can try everything free for 14 days: the trial starts when you choose monthly or yearly. After that, keep it all with Kattmat: every feature, for as many cats as you have, including sharing with a partner and sitters and the sitter checklist PDF. Choose monthly or yearly, which renew until you cancel, or pay once for good. The App Store shows the prices in your currency.

Without a plan, everything you added stays in Kattmat to look at, back up and restore; choose a plan to tick things off, get reminders and add things again.

If someone invites you, you don't pay. A sitter uses Kattmat free for the days they look after the pets, and a partner for as long as they're in the household. To keep a household of your own afterwards, choose Kattmat.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases go through the App Store. To cancel a subscription or the trial period:

  1. In Kattmat, tap Settings.
  2. Tap your plan at the top: Trial period or Kattmat.
  3. Tap Manage subscription.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial period ends and you pay nothing for it. A one-time purchase has nothing to cancel.

Refunds go through Apple, not Kattmat:

  1. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Tap I’d like to, then Request a refund.
  3. Choose why, then tap Next.
  4. Choose Kattmat, then tap Submit.

Privacy

Kattmat keeps your household on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn it on, and collects nothing. The details are in the privacy policy.
