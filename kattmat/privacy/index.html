Privacy policy

Kattmat for iPhone · Last updated 10 November 2026

Hi, Ruby here. The short version: Kattmat keeps your household on your iPhone, and we never see it. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What Kattmat stores

Your pets, their routines and what's been marked done, weigh-ins, care guides, notes, and vet and emergency contacts. If you share your household, also the names of the people in it, a sitter's days, and who marked each thing done or wrote each note. All of it is stored in the app on your iPhone. Kattmat has no server, and it sends none of this to us. It reaches other people only when you share it, as below.

If you turn on iCloud sync, your household is also stored in your own private iCloud, so it's on every device signed in to your Apple Account. Only your Apple Account can read it, until you share it; we can't. Apple's push service tells your devices when something changed. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

If you share your household, the people you invite get it in their Kattmat, through Apple's iCloud sharing. The invite shows them your name and your pets before they join. A partner gets the whole household on their iPhone (pets, routines, History, weigh-ins, care guides, notes, contacts and who's in it) and sees and can change all of it. A sitter gets only what their screens show, in a space of their own: the care guides and contacts of the pets they look after, the routines, and what was marked done and written on their days. Not your older History, weigh-ins, pets in memory, your Kattmat plan, other sitters' notes or anyone's Apple Account. They can mark routines done, undo their own ticks and write notes; what they do reaches your household only through your or your partner's iPhone, which takes in only that, with their name. Apple's sharing shows a sitter the names of the people their space is shared with (you and your partner), with an identifier Apple keeps for each account; Kattmat can't hide those. A sitter's access ends after the last day you chose, when you or your partner end it, or when they leave. A partner's ends when you remove them or they leave. We never see a shared household either.

Once a partner has joined, your Kattmat plan (which one, and when it ends) is saved in the shared household too, so their iPhone knows whether it may invite a sitter.

For a sitter without Kattmat, Kattmat makes a checklist PDF with the care guide, the contacts and a box for each routine. You send it yourself, and they send the filled-in copy back to you; Kattmat reads what was done and the notes on your iPhone. The file goes only where you and they send it.

What we collect

Nothing. Kattmat has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't ask to track you.

If you choose to share crash reports or usage data with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple collects them and shows us summaries without your name. That is Apple's, under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders, Siri and widgets

Reminders, the one before your trial period ends included, are planned on your iPhone; Kattmat has no push server of its own. Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and the Control Center control are run by iOS on your iPhone. What Siri does with your voice is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Backups

When you back up your household, Kattmat makes one file and you choose where it goes: Files, AirDrop, Mail. It stays wherever you put it. Kattmat also keeps a few automatic backups on your iPhone.

Purchases

Kattmat (monthly, yearly or once for good) and treats for Ruby are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Kattmat only learns which plan you have and when it ends, and checks it on your iPhone. We never see your payment details.

Writing to us

If you email us, we get your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you, keep it no longer than we need to, and never share it.

Testing a beta. If you test Kattmat through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us the name and email address we invited you with (nothing if you joined through a public link), your device model and iOS version, how often you open the beta, crash reports, and any feedback and screenshots you send. We use them only to fix Kattmat, and never share them. Within 30 days after the beta ends, or after you ask, we delete what we hold: you as a tester, your feedback and screenshots, and your emails to us. Apple keeps its own copy for a time under its TestFlight privacy terms, which we can't delete; you can manage it at privacy.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Delete Kattmat from your iPhone and everything it stored there goes with it. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them.

When a sitter's access ends, Kattmat removes the household from their iPhone; what they marked done and wrote stays in your History.

When a partner leaves or you remove them, their iPhone goes back to its own household, and their ticks and notes stay in your History. Kattmat keeps no copy of your household on their iPhone: none of its automatic backups from while they were in it stay.

To delete your household from iCloud too, open Settings in Kattmat and tap Delete household from iCloud… (on the household owner's iPhone, while Keep in iCloud is on). Everything stays on your iPhone, and sharing with anyone you invited ends. You can also use Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage on your iPhone.

Children

Kattmat is fine for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. For Kattmat that's at most an email you sent us, and if you test a beta, what TestFlight shares with us (above). You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

If Kattmat ever starts handling data differently, this page changes first, with a new date at the top.
